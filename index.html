Microcontrollers & Sensor Logging · 2026

Adafruit Feather RP2040 Adalogger
Arduino IDE
What we're building: A pocket-sized environmental data logger that measures light, temperature, and humidity, saves readings to an SD card, and runs on a battery in the field. Students build it from scratch over two days — one module at a time.
